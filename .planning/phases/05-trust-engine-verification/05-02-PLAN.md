<objective>
Build the trust/verification engine core: server-computed weight, the concurrency-safe atomic two-verification publish, numeric confidence authority, trust-event append, the impact-count increment, and the notification-outbox enqueue.

Purpose: This is the single most safety-critical surface in the phase. It must never double-publish, never trust client-derived values, silently zero shadowbanned influence, and keep every publish side-effect in one atomic transaction.

Output: `phase5_confidence_numeric`, `phase5_app_config_seeds`, `phase5_notification_outbox`, and `phase5_verify_and_publish` migrations; `verify_location` RPC; `submit_location` update to write creator weight-inputs; two pgTAP suites; regenerated types. A decision checkpoint locks the trust delta table, confidence thresholds, and decay constants before they are written into migrations.
</objective>
