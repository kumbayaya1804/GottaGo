### Round-3 Finding Resolution
- **FULLY RESOLVED.** `supabase/migrations/20260731000300_phase5_verify_and_publish.sql:167-181` now creates the per-user rate-limit row idempotently before attempting to lock it, then uses a lock/read/update statement that returns the prior timestamp. Concurrent first-use calls can no longer both observe an absent row: the conflicting insert and subsequent row lock serialize them, and the loser evaluates the winner's committed claim before proceeding.
- Keeping transaction-scoped `v_now` instead of `clock_timestamp()` is acceptable for this RPC. After serialization, an equal or even earlier frozen timestamp still makes the second call satisfy the `< cooldown` rejection condition, so it cannot reopen the acceptance bypass. A long client transaction or lock wait can conservatively extend throttling, but that is a bounded false-denial/UX edge rather than a security or integrity bypass, and no queued caller invokes the RPC twice in an externally managed transaction.
- RACE 5 meets the evidentiary bar for this repair. Its back-to-back asynchronous dispatch does not mechanically assert overlap on every possible scheduler run, but the exact queued test passed on the fixed body and failed when I restored the vulnerable pre-fix select-then-upsert body: assertions 89-90 observed `have: 2`, `want: 1`. That controlled RED/GREEN mutation proves the test reached the vulnerable overlap in this review and distinguishes the repaired implementation from the known defect. A separate blocking-handshake fixture is not required for approval.
