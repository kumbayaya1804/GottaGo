### Findings

#### 1. MAJOR - The working-tree limitation is incomplete and overstates which archives are index-bound

`docs/agent-harness.md:182-187` says the decision-bearing working-tree inputs are policy, calibration contract/evidence, packets, and canonical verdicts, and then states that archived verdicts are bound to indexed blobs. The actual hook has two additional distinctions that this disclosure omits:

- `.claude/review-queue.txt` is read from the working tree by `readLines()` at `.claude/hooks/check-review-artifacts.js:547-549`; its entries drive both the queued-file decision and `stagedScopeHash()` at lines 551-553 and 617-630. An unstaged queue edit can therefore change what the gate evaluates without changing what the commit records.
- Calibration prompt files and calibration `verdictArchive` files are also read directly from the working tree at `.claude/hooks/check-review-artifacts.js:263-280`. `validateCalibration()` never calls `stagedBlobOid()` or `workingTreeBlobOid()`. The OID/index-membership comparison at lines 359-382 is used only by `hasArchivedCopy()` for the current canonical reviewer verdicts at lines 755-757, not for calibration receipt archives.

This matters because the new section is meant to disclose the remaining approval-integrity boundary accurately. As written, a future maintainer can reasonably conclude that every verdict archive is guaranteed to enter the commit and that the review queue is outside the working-tree mismatch, when neither conclusion is true.

Required fix: revise limitation 1 to name the working-tree review queue and calibration prompt/archive receipt inputs, and narrow the archive claim to the exact implemented guarantee: current canonical verdict archives checked through `hasArchivedCopy()` are OID-bound to the index; calibration receipt archives are content-address/path/verdict checked but are not required to be present in the index. Alternatively, extend the hook and tests to provide the broader guarantee before retaining the current prose.
