### Adversarial Disproof

- **Disproof Attempt 1 (Bypassing Name Validation with Unicode Whitespace)**:
  - Test: In PostgreSQL POSIX regex, `[:space:]` encompasses standard ASCII and multibyte whitespace. `p_name !~ '[^[:space:]]'` evaluates whether there exists at least one character that is *not* a member of the space class. A string composed solely of whitespace fails the negation and raises `invalid submission`. Tested with space, tab (`chr(9)`), and newline/carriage return sequences (`chr(10) || ' ' || chr(13)`). All were rejected.
- **Disproof Attempt 2 (Corrupting PostGIS with NaN or Out-Of-Bounds Coordinates)**:
  - Test: Evaluated whether `extensions.st_makepoint(p_lng, p_lat)` could be reached with NaN or values > 90 / > 180. Because the numeric check precedes the insert statement, execution halts with `gps rejected`. No coordinate wrapping or PostGIS geography instantiation occurs.
