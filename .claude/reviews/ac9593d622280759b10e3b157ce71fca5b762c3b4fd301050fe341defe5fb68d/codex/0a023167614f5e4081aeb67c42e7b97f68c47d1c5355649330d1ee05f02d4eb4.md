### Findings

#### 1. MAJOR - The revised sentence still attributes the OID binding to the canonical `*-latest.md` paths instead of their archive copies

`docs/agent-harness.md:188-190` now says the current canonical reviewer verdicts, explicitly naming `.claude/antigravity-review-latest.md` and `.claude/codex-review-latest.md`, are OID-bound to the index through `hasArchivedCopy()`. They are not. The hook reads each canonical path from the working tree at `.claude/hooks/check-review-artifacts.js:694`, then `hasArchivedCopy()` constructs a separate content-addressed `.claude/reviews/<scope>/<reviewer>/<sha256>.md` path at lines 359-368 and compares the index and working-tree OIDs of that archive path at lines 381-382. It never compares the canonical `*-latest.md` path's index OID.

The distinction is observable in the live review state: both canonical latest files currently have index OIDs different from their working-tree OIDs, while their staged content-addressed archive copies are the mechanism the gate accepts. The paragraph's final statement at lines 193-195 is correspondingly too broad for canonical verdict content: an unstaged canonical-path edit is not wholly absent from the commit when the gate passes, because a byte-identical archive copy of the reviewed working-tree content must be staged; only the canonical pathname can retain different committed bytes.

This is the exact approval-integrity mechanism the new section exists to document, so retaining the pathname/content conflation would leave the central claim inaccurate.

Required fix: state that only the byte-identical content-addressed archive copies of the current canonical verdict contents are OID-bound to the index through `hasArchivedCopy()`; the canonical `*-review-latest.md` files themselves remain working-tree reads and are not index-compared. Adjust the concluding sentence to distinguish that archived content capture from the other working-tree-only inputs, which have no equivalent indexed copy requirement. The Round 2 additions covering the gitignored review queue and unstaged calibration receipt archives should remain.
