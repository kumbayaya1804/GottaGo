- [MAJOR] `.claude/hooks/check-review-artifacts.js:157` - The calibration gate accepts self-asserted counters and `{ caseId, passed: true }` rows but does not validate the receipts required by `ANTIGRAVITY.md:101`: model identity, prompt hashes, verdict archive paths, commands, timestamps, or independently distinguishable runs. - [MAJOR] `.claude/hooks/check-review-artifacts.js:183` - The archive check does not enforce the documented content-addressed path or immutable history.
