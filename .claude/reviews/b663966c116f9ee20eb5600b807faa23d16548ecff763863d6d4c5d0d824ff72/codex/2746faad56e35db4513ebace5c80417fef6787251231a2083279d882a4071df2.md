#### 1. MAJOR - The concluding sentence still contradicts the newly-correct archive guarantee

`docs/agent-harness.md:188-198` now correctly distinguishes the canonical `*-review-latest.md` pathname from the content-addressed archive copy, but lines 198-200 immediately say that an unstaged edit to “any” listed working-tree input is invisible to what a commit records. That is not true for canonical verdict content when the gate passes: `hasArchivedCopy()` requires a byte-identical, content-addressed archive copy in the index. The canonical pathname can commit older/different bytes, but the reviewed working-tree content is still recorded elsewhere in the same commit.

The live Round 2 artifact demonstrates the distinction directly. `.claude/codex-review-latest.md` has tracked/index blob `d028427...` while its current working-tree blob is `29c0d22...`; the staged archive `.claude/reviews/ac9593d.../codex/0a023167....md` also has blob `29c0d22...` and byte-identical SHA-256. The canonical path differs, but its reviewed content is not invisible to the commit.

There is also a smaller call-path attribution error at lines 189-191: `hasArchivedCopy()` does not itself read the canonical verdict from the working tree. The caller reads it with `readFileSafe(req.file)` at `.claude/hooks/check-review-artifacts.js:694` and passes the content at lines 755-757; `hasArchivedCopy()` reads only the derived archive path at line 370.

Required fix: rewrite the final sentence to distinguish canonical verdicts from the other inputs—for example, state that the canonical pointer pathname may commit different bytes while a byte-identical archive captures the reviewed verdict content, whereas the queue, policy, calibration inputs, and packets have no equivalent indexed-copy guarantee. Also change the helper description to say the gate/caller reads canonical content and passes it to `hasArchivedCopy()`, which derives and checks the archive path. Preserve the now-correct queue, calibration-receipt, and pointer-versus-archive distinctions.
